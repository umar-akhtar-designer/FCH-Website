Free Consultation

Everything starts with a no-obligation, 30-minute one-to-one session with one of our certified counsellors. This is your chance to share your academic background, your goals, your budget, and your preferred destination — and for us to give you an honest, expert assessment of your options.

02
Week 1–2

Profile Evaluation & Planning

After your consultation, your assigned counsellor conducts a thorough profile evaluation. We review every aspect of your academic history, language test scores, work experience, and financial standing — identifying strengths to highlight and gaps to close before applying.

You receive a written profile report with a clear action plan: which tests to take, what scores you need, which financial documents to prepare, and a timeline for every milestone.

Full academic transcript analysis
English test score requirements mapped
Financial eligibility check per destination
Written action plan with deadlines
03
Week 2–3

University Shortlisting

Using our 6-factor matching system, we build a personalised shortlist of 5–8 universities from our network of 500+ partners. Each university on your list is ranked by acceptance likelihood, scholarship availability, programme quality, and career outcomes.

5–8 personalised university recommendations
QS / Times ranking comparison sheet
Tuition fee and living cost breakdown
Scholarship options per university
Entry requirements confirmed before applying
Graduate employment rates reviewed
04
Week 3–6

Application & Offer Letter

Our admissions team completes every part of your applications — UCAS (UK), Common App (USA), OUAC (Canada), or direct university portals. We prepare your SOP, organise all supporting documents, and submit everything before the deadline.

We then monitor all applications and liaise directly with university admissions offices on your behalf — chasing updates, responding to queries, and negotiating conditional offer conditions where possible.

Complete application form management
SOP / personal statement crafted by experts
All documents formatted and verified
Offer letter review and condition guidance
05
Week 6–10

Visa Documentation & Submission

Once you hold an offer letter, our visa specialists build your application file. Every document is reviewed, every bank statement verified, every translated certificate formatted to the exact standard required. We then submit the application and keep you updated until the decision arrives.

Standard Visa Document Checklist

Valid passport (min. 6 months validity)
University offer / CAS / I-20 letter
IELTS / PTE score certificate
Bank statements (6 months + required balance)
Academic transcripts & degree certificates
Sponsor letter (if financially sponsored)
Visa application fee payment receipt
06
Week 10–14

Pre-Departure Briefing & Fly!

Your visa is approved — congratulations! But our job isn't done. We run a comprehensive pre-departure session covering everything you need to know before landing: accommodation setup, university enrolment, bank accounts, SIM cards, NHS registration (UK), healthcare cover, and connecting you with our alumni community in your destination city.

Full pre-departure briefing session

Airport arrival & first-week guide

Most universities have September (main) and January (winter) intakes. Some Canadian and Australian universities also have May intakes. We advise on the best intake based on your timeline.
⚡ Urgent Applications
Missed the standard deadline? We have express-application pathways for some of our partner universities and can submit applications for the next available intake within days.

No problem. You can apply to many universities with a conditional offer while you prepare for IELTS. Our in-house IELTS coaches can get you test-ready in 6–8 weeks.
