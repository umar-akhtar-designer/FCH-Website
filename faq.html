Visa Questions
For a UK Student visa you need: (1) A Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed UK university, (2) IELTS Academic score of 6.0–7.0 depending on the university, (3) Proof of sufficient funds — typically £1,334/month for London or £1,023/month outside London for living costs, plus first year tuition, (4) Valid passport, and (5) UKVI-approved English test result. Our visa team prepares every document to the exact UKVI standard.
Processing times vary by country: UK Student Visa typically takes 3 weeks (standard) or 5 working days (priority). Canada Study Permit takes 4–8 weeks. US F-1 Visa takes 1–4 weeks after embassy interview. Australia Subclass 500 typically takes 4–6 weeks. Germany Student Visa takes 6–12 weeks. We factor these timelines into your application schedule so you always apply well in advance.
Our 98% visa success rate means refusals are rare — but they do happen. If your visa is refused, we analyse the refusal letter in detail, address every concern the visa officer raised, and reapply. We also explore appeal options where available. We do not charge additional fees for a reapplication caused by document issues on our end.

IELTS / PTE Questions
Requirements vary by country and university level: UK undergraduate: typically 6.0–6.5 overall, UK postgraduate: 6.5–7.0 overall. Canada: 6.0–6.5. Australia: 6.0–7.0. USA: 6.5–7.5 depending on the university. Germany: 6.0–6.5 for English-taught programmes. Our counsellors will tell you the exact band required for your specific target universities during the free consultation.
Most students can improve their IELTS score by 0.5–1.5 bands in 6–8 weeks of focused preparation with our coaches. Students starting from a lower base (band 4–5) may need 3–4 months. Our certified IELTS trainers run structured programmes with mock tests, speaking practice, writing corrections, and exam strategy coaching — and our students average band 7.2 on their first attempt.
Yes — PTE Academic is widely accepted, particularly in Australia, Canada, and the UK. Many students find PTE easier and faster since it is computer-marked and results come within 5 business days. We offer full PTE Academic preparation alongside our IELTS programme. Your counsellor will advise which test is better suited to your profile and target universities.
Germany's public universities for English-taught programmes require IELTS, but German-taught programmes require German language (B2/C1) instead. Admissions Questions
We recommend applying to 5–8 universities — a mix of ambitious, realistic, and safe choices. This maximises your chance of getting multiple offers while keeping the process manageable. In the UK, UCAS allows up to 5 choices. For the USA and Canada, we typically apply to 6–10 institutions. Applying to the right universities is more important than applying to many — we use our profile-matching to ensure every application has a strong chance.
Standard requirements include: Academic transcripts and degree certificates, IELTS/PTE score certificate, passport copy, Statement of Purpose (SOP) / personal statement, 2–3 academic or professional references/recommendation letters, and CV/resume (for postgraduate). Some programmes also require a portfolio (architecture, design, arts) or GMAT/GRE scores (MBA, certain masters). We prepare and verify every document for you.
The SOP (also called a personal statement or motivation letter) is your opportunity to tell the university who you are, why you want to study this specific programme, and how it fits your career goals. It is extremely important — for many competitive programmes it carries more weight than grades. A poorly written SOP can result in rejection despite excellent grades. Our team of former university admissions readers craft SOPs with a 94% acceptance rate at target universities.
Deadlines vary by country: UK UCAS: 31 January for most courses, 15 October for Oxford/Cambridge and medicine. USA: Early Decision (November), Regular Decision (January–February). Canada: Varies by university — typically November–February for September intake. Australia: Rolling admissions, typically 3–4 months before intake. Germany: 15 July for winter semester (October start), 15 January for summer semester. We track every deadline and submit well in advance.
Costs & Scholarships
Total annual costs (tuition + living) vary widely: UK: £20,000–£40,000/year. USA: $35,000–$70,000/year. Canada: CAD $25,000–$50,000/year. Australia: AUD $35,000–$65,000/year. Germany: €8,000–€16,000/year (living only — most public tuition is free!). Malaysia: MYR 30,000–80,000/year (very affordable). We provide a detailed cost breakdown for every university on your shortlist during our consultation.
Many scholarships are available: Chevening Scholarship (UK — full funding including tuition, living, and flights). Commonwealth Scholarships (UK). DAAD Scholarships (Germany — fully funded). Australia Awards (full funding). HEC Overseas Scholarships (Pakistani government — covers tuition and living). University merit scholarships (20–100% tuition reduction). Need-based bursaries. Our scholarship guidance service identifies every scholarship you qualify for and manages the entire application process.
