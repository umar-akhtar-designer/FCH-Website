Visa Counselling

Our visa counselling service is the foundation of everything we do. With a 98% approval rate across UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, and more, our certified counsellors know exactly what visa officers look for — and how to present your file perfectly.

We don't just fill forms. We build a complete, cohesive visa narrative — ensuring your financial documents, intention letter, and supporting evidence all tell a compelling, consistent story.

1
Initial Assessment — We review your profile, financials, ties to home country, and travel history to identify any risk areas before submission.
2
Document Checklist — Every required document listed and verified against the latest official guidance for your specific visa category.
3
File Preparation — We review, correct, and organise every document. Translated certificates, bank statements, offer letters — all formatted to specification.
4
Interview Coaching — For countries requiring an embassy interview (USA, Germany), we run mock sessions until you are fully confident.
5
Submission & Tracking — We submit your application and monitor every update until the decision arrives.

University Selection

Choosing the right university is the single most important decision in your study-abroad journey. Get it wrong and you waste years and thousands of pounds. Get it right and you unlock your entire future career.

Our counsellors use a structured 6-factor matching system — grades, budget, course content, career goal, location preference, and scholarship potential — to build your personalised shortlist from our network of 500+ partner universities.

Personalised shortlist of 5–8 universities matched to your exact profile

Service 03

Admissions Assistance

Missing a deadline or submitting an incomplete application can cost you an entire intake year. Our admissions team manages your entire application — from form-filling and document verification to direct liaison with university admissions offices.

1
Application Portal Setup — We create and set up your accounts on UCAS, Common App, OUAC, or individual university portals.
2
Form Completion — Every section completed accurately — academic history, personal details, employment, references.
3
Document Upload — Transcripts, degree certificates, IELTS scores, passport copies — all formatted and uploaded correctly.
4
Offer Letter Management — We review conditional/unconditional offers and advise on meeting conditions and accepting the right one.
5
CAS / I-20 Coordination — We liaise with the university to secure your CAS number (UK) or I-20 (USA) for visa application.
