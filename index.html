Step by Step

Visa Approval
Timeline

From the moment you decide to study abroad to the day you board your flight — we handle every step. Here's exactly what happens and when.

98% Visa Success Rate
Avg. 8–12 Weeks
Student Submits Application Week 1

You share your academic documents, IELTS score, and preferred universities with our team.

Offer Letter Received Week 2–3

University confirms your seat and issues an official conditional or unconditional offer letter.

CAS Issued Week 4–5

Confirmation of Acceptance to Studies (CAS) number issued by your university — required for visa application.

Visa Filed Week 6

Our team prepares and submits your visa application with all supporting documents — bank statements, accommodation, and travel history.

Visa Approved ✓ Week 8–10

You receive your visa — 98% of our students get approved first time. We notify you and guide the next steps.

Departure Day 🎉 Week 10–12

Pre-departure briefing done, accommodation confirmed, bags packed. Safe travels — your future starts now.
